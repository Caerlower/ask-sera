# gSera and community rewards

**gSera** is Sera’s **loyalty / community points** system — not the settlement smart-contract token that powers custody, and not required to swap.

## What gSera is

Public ambassador / community copy describes gSera as:

- Loyalty points earned for **real trades**, **liquidity**, and **people you bring** (referrals / network effects)
- Used for rewards and rank in the community program
- Part of a broader rewards stack:
  - **gSera** — loyalty points (described as live)
  - **Stable rewards** — “real money” rewards (publicly framed as rolling out, e.g. Q3 2026 messaging — subject to change)
  - **XP & badges** — program rank, multipliers, access

## What gSera is NOT

- Not required for `GET /config`, vault deposits, or EIP-712 swap/order signing
- Not a substitute for USDC/USDT/etc. settlement assets
- Not something Ask Sera can “claim” or transfer — point users to the official gSera / ambassador dashboard

## Related programs

- **Ambassador program** — [ambassador.sera.cx](https://ambassador.sera.cx): community / evangelist / builder tracks; XP tiers; career pipeline messaging
- Marketing site historically referenced **gSera pre-registration** (cohorts may close — always check the live site)

## Answering users

If asked “what is the gSera token?”:

1. Explain it as **community loyalty points**, not the FX settlement asset.
2. Say economic details (claim amounts, conversion, TGE) only if present in retrieved knowledge; otherwise say those are announced on official Sera channels and may change.
3. Do not invent contract addresses or claim gSera is live on Uniswap / etc. without a grounded source.
